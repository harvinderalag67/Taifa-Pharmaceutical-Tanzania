WHO WE ARE

A Vision for Tanzania's Pharmaceutical Future

Taifa Pharmaceutical Tanzania Ltd. is a Tanzanian pharmaceutical company established with the vision of developing a modern, internationally compliant pharmaceutical manufacturing platform capable of producing high-quality medicines for Tanzania and the wider African region.

Our long-term objective is to strengthen national medicine security by reducing dependence on imported pharmaceuticals while supporting economic growth, industrial development, skilled employment, and regional healthcare resilience through local manufacturing.

Guided by international quality standards and a commitment to sustainable investment, Taifa Pharmaceutical Tanzania is building a manufacturing platform designed to meet WHO Good Manufacturing Practice (WHO-GMP) requirements and serve both public and private healthcare markets.

Beyond manufacturing medicines, our ambition is to contribute meaningfully to Tanzania's industrial transformation by creating a pharmaceutical enterprise recognised for quality, innovation, integrity and long-term value creation.

OUR STORY

From Vision to Reality

Taifa Pharmaceutical Tanzania was founded on the belief that every nation should possess the capability to manufacture safe, high-quality medicines for its people. Our journey is driven by a commitment to industrial excellence, healthcare resilience, and long-term national development.

The Vision

To establish one of East Africa's leading pharmaceutical manufacturing platforms, built on quality, innovation and international standards.

The Opportunity

Tanzania's growing healthcare needs, expanding pharmaceutical market and strategic regional position provide an exceptional opportunity for sustainable pharmaceutical manufacturing.

The Future

Our goal is to become a trusted pharmaceutical manufacturer serving Tanzania, the East African Community and wider African markets with internationally recognised quality.
