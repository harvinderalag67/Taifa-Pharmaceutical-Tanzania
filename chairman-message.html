CHAIRMAN'S MESSAGE

Building a Healthier Future for Tanzania

A message from the Chairman of Taifa Pharmaceutical Tanzania Ltd.

Rostam Aziz

Chairman

Taifa Pharmaceutical Tanzania Ltd.

Welcome to Taifa Pharmaceutical Tanzania Ltd.

It gives me immense pride to share our vision for what we believe will be a transformative chapter in Tanzania's healthcare and industrial development.

Across the world, access to quality medicines remains a critical pillar of public health. For many developing nations, dependence on imported pharmaceuticals has created vulnerabilities in supply chains, affordability, and long-term healthcare security. Tanzania, like many countries across Africa, has made significant progress in expanding healthcare access, yet the need for stronger local pharmaceutical manufacturing capacity has never been more urgent.

Taifa Pharmaceutical Tanzania Ltd. was established with a clear purpose: to contribute meaningfully to Tanzania's healthcare self-reliance by creating a world-class pharmaceutical manufacturing platform that serves our nation and the wider region.

Our vision extends beyond building a manufacturing facility. We are building an institution that will help strengthen national healthcare security, reduce dependence on imported medicines, support industrial development, create highly skilled employment opportunities, and contribute to Tanzania's long-term economic transformation.

The pharmaceutical manufacturing facility currently under development in Bagamoyo represents a strategic investment in Tanzania's future. Designed to meet international quality standards and guided by global best practices in pharmaceutical engineering, quality systems, and regulatory compliance, the project is intended to position Tanzania as an emerging pharmaceutical manufacturing hub for East Africa and beyond.

We recognize that healthcare is not simply about products; it is about people. It is about ensuring that patients have timely access to safe, effective, and affordable medicines. It is about empowering healthcare professionals with reliable therapeutic solutions. It is about supporting governments in achieving their healthcare objectives and improving the quality of life of their citizens.

As we progress through the construction, qualification, validation, and regulatory approval phases of this project, we remain committed to the highest standards of integrity, transparency, quality, and accountability. We are equally committed to investing in Tanzanian talent, developing local technical expertise, and creating opportunities for the next generation of scientists, pharmacists, engineers, and healthcare professionals.

The journey we have undertaken is ambitious, but ambition is necessary when the objective is national progress. We firmly believe that Tanzania possesses the talent, determination, and vision required to build a globally respected pharmaceutical manufacturing sector capable of serving both domestic and international markets.

OUR GUIDING PHILOSOPHY
"Vision brings you to the mountain. Strategy gets you to the top."

That philosophy continues to inspire every decision we make as we work towards building a healthcare manufacturing platform that will create lasting value for patients, healthcare systems, communities, investors, employees, and future generations.

I invite you to follow our journey and become part of a shared vision to strengthen healthcare access, support economic development, and help position Tanzania as a leader in pharmaceutical manufacturing excellence.

Together, we are building more than a pharmaceutical facility.

We are building a healthier future for Tanzania.

Rostam Aziz

Chairman
Taifa Pharmaceutical Tanzania Ltd.
