OUR QUALITY PHILOSOPHY

Quality Is Designed In.
Not Inspected In.

At Taifa, quality is intended to be built into the manufacturing platform from the beginning, rather than treated as a final inspection activity.

The approach brings together facility design, controlled processes, trained people, documented systems, qualification, validation and laboratory controls within one connected quality framework.

The objective is straightforward: establish the systems and controls needed to produce medicines consistently, while building the foundation for regulatory readiness and future regional expansion.

01

Quality by Design

Quality considerations begin with the facility, equipment, processes and systems that support pharmaceutical manufacturing.

02

Controlled & Documented

Defined processes, documented systems and appropriate controls provide the foundation for consistent manufacturing performance.

03

Qualified & Validated

Equipment, systems and processes are intended to progress through appropriate qualification and validation activities.

04

Continuously Strengthened

Monitoring, evidence and learning provide the basis for identifying opportunities to strengthen processes and quality performance.
